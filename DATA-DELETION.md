# Data Deletion Request

**DTech-Africa** - User Data Deletion Instructions

## Overview

This page provides instructions for users who wish to request the deletion of their personal data from DTech-Africa services and applications, including those integrated with Facebook Platform.

## How to Request Data Deletion

If you would like to delete your data from our systems, please follow these steps:

### Method 1: Email Request (Recommended)

Send an email to our privacy team with your deletion request:

**Email:** privacy@dtech-africa.org

**Subject:** Data Deletion Request

**Required Information:**
- Your full name
- Email address associated with your account
- Facebook User ID (if applicable)
- Any usernames or account identifiers
- Description of which service(s) you used
- Confirmation that you want all your data deleted

**Example Email:**
```
Subject: Data Deletion Request

Dear DTech-Africa Privacy Team,

I am requesting the deletion of all my personal data from your systems.

Name: [Your Name]
Email: [Your Email]
Facebook User ID: [Your Facebook ID if applicable]
Services Used: [List the DTech-Africa services you used]

I confirm that I want all my personal data deleted from your systems.

Thank you,
[Your Name]
```

### Method 2: GitHub Issue

You can also submit a data deletion request by creating an issue in our privacy repository:

1. Go to https://github.com/DTECH-AFRI/privacy/issues
2. Click "New Issue"
3. Title: "Data Deletion Request - [Your Name]"
4. Include the required information listed above
5. Submit the issue

## What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request data deletion, we will remove:

- Your account information (name, email, username, password)
- Your profile information and settings
- Content you created or uploaded
- Usage logs and activity data
- Any other personal information associated with your account
- Data received from Facebook Platform (if applicable)

## Data Deletion Timeline

- **Acknowledgment:** We will acknowledge your request within **48 hours**
- **Completion:** Data deletion will be completed within **30 days** of your request
- **Confirmation:** You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete

## What Data May Be Retained

For legal and security purposes, we may retain certain information for a limited time:

- Transaction records (as required by law)
- Legal compliance data (as required by regulations)
- Anonymized analytics data that cannot identify you
- Backup copies (which will be deleted during regular backup cycles, typically within 90 days)

## Facebook Integration

If you used DTech-Africa services through Facebook:

- Your data deletion request will also remove data we received from Facebook
- You may also want to revoke DTech-Africa app access in your Facebook Settings
- Go to Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → Remove "DTech-Africa"

## Your Rights

Under data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:

- **Access** your data
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data
- **Export** your data
- **Object** to processing of your data
